# Nautilus

A modern offline-first writing studio for authors.

Nautilus is a desktop writing application designed for storytellers, novelists, worldbuilders, and beginner writers who want a calm, beautiful place to write.

It combines:

- the speed and ownership of local-first tools,
- the structure of professional writing software,
- and the simplicity of modern creative apps.

No accounts. No mandatory cloud sync. No clutter.

Just open Nautilus and start writing.

## Vision

Most writing software today suffers from at least one major issue:

- expensive pricing models,
- outdated user experience,
- overwhelming complexity,
- or cloud-only workflows.

Nautilus exists to solve that.

The goal is to create a writing environment that feels:

- calming,
- focused,
- modern,
- and emotionally safe for creators.

Nautilus is designed for people who want to:

- write novels,
- organize ideas,
- build worlds,
- track characters,
- and actually finish stories.
